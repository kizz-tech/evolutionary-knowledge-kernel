# Experience, current understanding and useful action

EKK asks whether a personal agent environment and shared work can accumulate
methods whose usefulness survives changes in participant, model and conditions.
The person receives the benefit and chooses the purpose. Shared work preserves
continuity. Methods carry selected experience without requiring people to exchange
all their memory.

The concept also includes an epistemic loop: use preserved experience to form a
fallible view of the present, obtain observations that matter to the next
decision, act within current authority, and check what actually happened.
Competence includes recognizing when a previously useful method lacks sufficient
grounds for this action in this world. A complete context pack cannot establish
that sufficiency by itself.

## Knowledge and the present world

Keep five meanings distinct:

| Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- |
| World | The selected environment actually runs a particular schema version. |
| Observation | A named sensor reported that version for that environment at a particular time. |
| Belief | Given available evidence, the participant expects the required schema to be present; another explanation may remain possible. |
| Knowledge | Preserved sources, observations, interpretations and experience explain how that view was reached and revised. |
| Commitment | The change requires a particular schema and may be attempted only within separately granted authority. |

EKK preserves the grounds and commitments. A **Belief Runtime** derives a temporary,
action-scoped working view from authorized knowledge and current observations.
The host owns sensors, capabilities and execution authority. The world remains
with its owning systems. A belief snapshot is a historical account of what was
considered at a time; loading it cannot make those conditions current again.

The working question is: **what must be established for the next action, what
remains uncertain, and which available observation could change the decision?**
Sometimes the right next step is an observation; sometimes it is action with
adequate grounds; sometimes the necessary information is unavailable. A named
uncertainty with no bearing on the decision need not stop useful work.

This direction is part of the current concept. The
[Belief Runtime contract](belief-runtime.md) separates that design choice from
implemented mechanisms and unmeasured benefit. It does not introduce a second
canonical knowledge base or change `ekk.record/0.1`.

## Fallibility and authority

The person can choose a purpose while being mistaken about facts, causes or their
own understanding. Models and evaluators can also err. Source provenance, recorded
interpretation, local acceptance and permission to act are separate contracts.
Neither human confirmation nor AI authorship establishes truth. Corrections and
explicit disagreement preserve the original bytes and the exact disputed version.

A personal draft can contain doubt or disagreement without becoming a project
commitment. A project commitment can remain valid and available after its author
leaves. Reading either does not transfer ownership or establish understanding.

## Two coupled timescales

The fast loop is observation → revised working belief → action-readiness
assessment → authorized attempt → separately observed outcome. An attempt can
finish while its effect or outcome remains unknown. A confidence label, an exit
code or an orchestration receipt does not complete that loop.

The slower learning loop is experience → candidate method → independent evaluation →
local admission → use → permitted transfer → receiver evaluation → reconsideration.
Sometimes the correct final step is retirement, or no new method at all. More
instructions and memories do not imply greater capability.

Significant observations, unsuccessful attempts and useful revisions can become
durable experience. The fast loop does not require storing every intermediate
thought. The slow loop does not make a historical conclusion a current-world
fact. Their connection is a research question about better decisions and useful
learning, including their full maintenance and investigation costs.

## Current evidence

The current runnable example starts with a method that includes a source excerpt
in a handoff. A later disclosure condition makes that method unsuitable. A
failed case is retained, use is quarantined, local acceptance is superseded, and
a new revision uses an allowed reference and digest. Original bytes and the
adverse result remain reconstructable.

That is a deterministic mechanism demonstration. It does not establish LLM
learning, human understanding or a causal productivity effect. The active study
compares a strong engineered baseline, personal memory, shared memory/methods,
and the combined approach under equal full-lifecycle budgets. It tests related
held-out tasks and harmful obsolete methods, rather than replaying a learned
answer. Human reconstruction and challenge of the grounds are measured separately.

The research is `designed_not_run`. No claim of scientific novelty, universal
scalability, monotonic improvement or general superiority follows from these
mechanisms. [Architecture](final-architecture.md) and the
[study contract](../research/studies/human-shared-method-transfer/README.md)
state what is implemented and what still requires empirical observation.
